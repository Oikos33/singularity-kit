FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need internet to use this after purchase?

No. The entire vault is self-contained on the drive. Once you have it, it works completely offline.

What power does the drive require?

Physical drives are USB-powered (5V) and can run from any solar-charged battery bank, hand-crank generator, or reactor system.

What is the annual update service?

Each year our team curates new scientific breakthroughs, updated survival protocols, and expanded databases. You receive only new content — no need to replace your drive.

What is the Infinite Water (Mugen Mizu) system?

Our proprietary compilation of atmospheric water generation, fog collection, deep-well construction, biological filtration, and solar desalination — to produce potable water from virtually any environment without grid infrastructure.

Can I load the content onto my own drive?

Yes. The SingularityKit is digital-only — download it and store it on any drive you own. Higher tiers include hardened pre-loaded drives, but you can always make additional copies.

Is this legal to own and distribute?

Yes. All content is sourced from open-access, public domain, and Creative Commons repositories. You own the data outright upon purchase.

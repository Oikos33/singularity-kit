FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need internet to use this after purchase?

No. The entire vault is self-contained on the drive. Once you have it, it works completely offline — designed precisely for scenarios where the internet is gone.

What power does the drive require?

Physical drives are USB-powered (5V) and can run from any solar-charged battery bank, hand-crank generator, or off-grid power source. The Genesis Pack includes a 12V adapter.

What is the annual update service?

Each year our team curates new scientific breakthroughs, updated survival protocols, and expanded databases. You receive a delta package with only new content — no need to replace your drive.

Can I load the content onto my own drive?

Yes. The SingularityKit is digital-only — download it and store it on any drive you own. Higher tiers include hardened pre-loaded drives, but you can always make additional copies.

Is this legal to own and distribute?

Yes. All content is sourced from open-access, public domain, and Creative Commons repositories. We compile, curate, and organize — you own the data outright upon purchase.
